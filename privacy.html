Romeflix privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Romeflix is a private app used by one family. When you sign in with Google, Romeflix asks only for read-only access to your YouTube account (youtube.readonly). It uses that access for one thing: showing the newest videos from the channels you subscribe to, and YouTube search results, on your Apple TV.

  • Romeflix cannot post, like, comment, subscribe, or change anything in your account.
  • Your sign-in is stored only on the family's own Mac that runs Romeflix. It is not sent to anyone else.
  • Romeflix does not sell, share, or use your data for advertising, and keeps no copy of your viewing history outside the Apple TV.
  • You can sign out at any time in Romeflix's Settings, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Romeflix's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
